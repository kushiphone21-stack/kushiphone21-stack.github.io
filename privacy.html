Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

"news-channel" is a private automation tool used only by the owner of the तीन ख़बर · Three News channel to publish its own videos and reply to comments on its own YouTube channel. It is not offered to other users.

What the tool accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the tool uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload videos, captions and thumbnails to the owner's channel;
  • read public comments on the channel's own videos and post replies the owner has approved.

What we store

The tool keeps only the IDs of comments it has already processed, so it doesn't reply twice. Comment text is processed temporarily to draft replies and is not sold, shared or used for advertising. OAuth credentials are stored as encrypted secrets and used only to act on the owner's own channel.

Third parties

Comment text may be sent to an AI provider (Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude) solely to draft a reply for the owner to review. YouTube's own privacy policy applies to viewers: policies.google.com/privacy.

Google API Services

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
